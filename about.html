Who I am, and why I do this.

My name is Benjamin Toh, but most people call me Ben. I'm the founder and lead designer of M2D Interior Design in Singapore, and the second-generation owner of the carpentry workshop my father built.

I grew up around timber, tools and tradesmen. Before I could read a floor plan, I knew what good joinery felt like, and how much care went into a cabinet nobody would ever look inside.

I went on to read Mechanical Engineering at Nanyang Technological University, graduating with Honours (Distinction) in 2018. That training still shapes how I read drawings, quotes and site conditions: with structural rigour, and a habit of asking why something is built the way it is.

Through M2D, I design homes alongside the people who build them. Through Bentohinteriors, I share what I see on site, honestly. Through The Brief, I teach homeowners who want to renovate with their eyes open, and through its Practitioner Programme, aspiring designers who want to do this work properly. Changing lives, changing homes, changing how we live for the better. That is the whole point.

If you're planning a home, starting out as a designer, or simply curious about the craft, I'd love to hear from you.

What I believe

Four things I hold to.

  • I

    Education beats persuasion.

    A homeowner who understands a quotation can't be oversold. I would rather teach than pitch.

  • II

    Design should know how it's built.

    Drawings made far from the workshop cost clients money. Mine are made next to it.

  • III

    Craft is worth passing on.

    As making moves to machines, I want more people to have held a chisel at least once.

  • IV

    Better designers, better homes.

    The fastest way to raise the standard of homes is to raise the standard of the people designing them.
