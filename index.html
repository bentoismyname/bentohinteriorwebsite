Meet Ben

Engineer by training,
carpenter by upbringing,
designer by choice.

I grew up in my father’s carpentry workshop, and today I run it. That is where my standards come from: a home should still make sense long after the photos are taken.

I read mechanical engineering at NTU before founding M2D Interior Design. Bentohinteriors is my personal practice, where I design homes, review the ones you are about to buy, and teach homeowners what the industry rarely tells them.
